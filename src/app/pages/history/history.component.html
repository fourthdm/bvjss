Maharashtra is identified for robust peoples and culture along with many backward casts. In this developing Maharashtra, all backward cast are placed out of race. They can’t afford fundamental facilities like food, clothes and shelter as well as education.

In this situation Mr. Shivlal Jadhav came to Pune from his village to learn and to earn fame. He was also from poor family. His mother thought him to serve the needy children.
“Instead of doing a job that pays for leaving, you have to work for those children of backward casts, who are unable to complete there education. This children must complete there education and can live self dependent life. You to bring this student into the flow of education. ”

With this motive, mother of Mr. Shivlal Jadhav started the small school for orphan children at home in 1985. Sometimes she used to bring food from her neighbourhood. While travelling the journy of social service, she started the hermitage school at Wagholi near Pune.
