Residential School for Mentally Challenged Children.




This residential school, located in the rural area of Pabal-approximately 40 kilometers from Pune-was established with the vision to support children from poor farming families who are mentally challenged. The institution offers free accommodation, education, and care, aiming to uplift and empower children who are often marginalized and underserved.



Facilities and Support Services



The school is equipped with basic facilities and infrastructure, including dormitories, classrooms, a dining area, and recreational spaces.




It employs special educators and caregivers trained and certified by the Rehabilitation Council of India (RCI).






Children are provided with regular medical care, therapy, and counseling.




A strong emphasis is placed on sports, cultural activities, and value-based education to ensure all-round development.






Mission


The school is dedicated to providing a nurturing environment for mentally challenged children, ensuring their right to education, dignity, and personal growth.



Vision


It focuses on creating opportunities for these children to develop their physical, emotional, intellectual, and social abilities to the fullest extent possible.




Holistic Development Approach

Our focus extends beyond academic education. We aim to instill values of goodness, brotherhood, and kindness, helping children grow into responsible, compassionate, and capable citizens.


Education as a Tool for Empowerment

We believe that education is a powerful tool to break the cycle of poverty, especially for underprivileged children. In line with this belief, we work at the intersection of education and livelihood, preparing our students for employment and entrepreneurship.

Vocational Training Programs

We prepare our students for employment and entrepreneurship opportunities by working at the intersection of education and livelihood. To support this goal, we run three different vocational training courses in our Ashram.
