Our History


Maharashtra is known for its robust people and culture along with many backward castes. In this developing Maharashtra, all backward castes are placed out of the race. They can’t afford fundamental facilities like food, clothes, and shelter, as well as education.



Our Objectives

  • To help a child without family.
  • Give education to one’s who really needed.
  • To empower children to become self-reliant.

Our Mission

To make a difference in the life of an orphan, single child, poor child, needy child, and divyang child.


Our Schools


  • Shri Muk-Badhir Vidyalaya (Aashram), Wagholi
  • Residential School for Divyang Students, Pabal
  • Sai Aanad Muk Badhir Vidyalaya, Wadegawhan
