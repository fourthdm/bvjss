Our Story


BVJSS stands for Bhatkya Vimukt Jati Shikshan Sanstha. BVJSS is a non-profit organization bringing hope to orphans, handicapped, deaf, and children with intellectual disabilities. Since 1987, BVJSS has been serving orphaned and divyang children.
Currently, 350 students are living in the hermitage.
