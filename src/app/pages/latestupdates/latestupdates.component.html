Republic Day Celebrated with Great Enthusiasm at Bhatkya Vimukt Jati Shikshan Sanstha, Flag Hoisting in the Presence of Distinguished Guests
At the primary and secondary Ashram School run by theBhatkya Vimukt Jati Shikshan Sanstha, Wagholi (Pune), the 77th Republic Day of India was celebrated with great enthusiasm on 26th January. On this occasion, the national flag was hoisted by the esteemed guest Shri Fatechandji Ranka.
Distinguished guests present for the program included Chandrakant Lodha (social worker), Captain Shriram Kadam (entrepreneur), Shantaram Bapu Katke (Deputy Sarpanch, Wagholi), Jayprakash Satav (Gram Panchayat Member, Wagholi), Mrs. Minakaki Satav (social worker, Wagholi), Sunil Lodha (social worker), Jagnathrao Veddi, Avijit Karamkar (Nihilent Company), Mayank Vijayvargi, and Manoj Khobre (EATON), among others.















Residential School for Intellectually Disabled Children, Pabal - Runners-Up
