Mr.Naval Ghule





I am someone who naturally prefers to work in a challenging role where I genuinely get to make a difference to people's lives I did not become a social worker for financial reward. I have passion, commitment and dedication for social work.

Walking the streets daily with open eyes and no headphones in my ears, I can see the injustice all around. And while I know I am not going to change the world, but I also know that I can make a difference in lives of some people on that street. That’s why I opted for social work, I understand the impact of such a poor and orphaned child and the challenges such a family faces. That is why I am helping as much as I can in the field of social welfare..
