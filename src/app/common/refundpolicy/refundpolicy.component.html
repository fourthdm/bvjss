Return & Refund Policy


1. Applicability

This policy applies when you make a payment (e.g., donation, purchase, registration) via Cashfree payment gateway on our website.

2. Donations & Services

As per standard NGO practices in India, donations-including those made via Cashfree-are non‑refundable and cannot be cancelled once the transaction is successful and confirmed. This helps us utilize your contribution immediately for our cause. Exceptions may be considered only in the case of:

  • Duplicate or accidental transactions
  • Unauthorized or fraudulent charges
  • Technical errors during payment processing

In such exceptional cases, please contact us promptly. If a refund is approved, it will be processed following the Cashfree refund protocol.

3. Refund Process via Cashfree

  • Refunds, if applicable, will be initiated to your original source account or payment method (e.g. bank, credit card, UPI).
  • Depending on the method, refunds may take 5-7 working days to reflect in your account.
  • Cashfree may classify refunds as Standard Refund (5-7 days), Instant Refund, or Batch Refund.
  • The transaction fee and GST charged by Cashfree at the time of original payment are non-reversible and will not be refunded.

4. Cancellation

No cancellations are permitted for donations, event registrations, or program fees once payment is made. All such contributions are final and committed to the mission.

5. Queries & Grievances

If you believe an unauthorized or incorrect transaction has occurred, please reach out within 48 hours at:

6. Policy Updates

BVJSS reserves the right to update this policy at any time. Any changes will be effective immediately upon posting here with a revised date.

7. Governing Law

This policy is governed by the laws of India and subject to jurisdiction in Pune, Maharashtra.
